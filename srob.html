Differential Equations
in Robotics

Why It Matters

The mathematics of motion

Every movement a robot makes — from joint rotation to drone stabilization — is governed by differential equations that describe how position, velocity, and force change continuously in time.

Differential equations play a vital role in robotics because they describe how a robot moves, changes position, controls speed, and responds to external forces.

Every robotic system involves continuously changing variables such as displacement, velocity, acceleration, torque, and angular position — all of which can be modelled using differential equations.

Robotics engineers use differential equations to design, simulate, and control robots accurately and efficiently. Without them, precise automated motion would simply not be possible.
